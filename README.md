# E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes an online retail e-commerce dataset using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, customer activity, product performance, and market concentration.

The objective was to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights and recommendations from a Business Analyst perspective.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis aims to answer key business questions:

- How much revenue did the business generate?
- How many orders, customers, and products were involved?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Are there signs of market concentration or seasonal demand?
- What business actions can be recommended based on the findings?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

**Dataset:** Online Retail / E-Commerce transaction dataset

The dataset contains transaction-level information such as:

- Invoice Number
- Stock Code
- Product Description
- Quantity
- Invoice Date
- Unit Price
- Customer ID
- Country
- Transaction Type
- Sales

A calculated **Sales** field was used for revenue analysis.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

A monthly revenue trend was created to identify changes in sales over time.

**Key finding:**

Revenue peaked in **November 2011 at approximately £1.39M** before dropping sharply in December.

---

### 2. Revenue by Country

A Top 10 Countries by Revenue analysis was created using a Pivot Table and bar chart.

**Key finding:**

The **United Kingdom generated approximately £8.89M**, representing about **91% of total revenue**.

This indicates a strong dependence on the UK market.

---

### 3. Revenue by Product

A Top 10 Products by Revenue analysis was created to identify the highest-performing products.

**Key finding:**

**HEART FILIGREE DOVE SMALL** generated approximately **£81.5K**, making it the highest-revenue product in the analyzed Top 10.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

### Market Concentration

The large contribution from the United Kingdom indicates that the business is highly dependent on one market.

### Seasonal Demand

Revenue increased significantly toward November and then dropped sharply in December, indicating a potential seasonal pattern.

### Product Performance

A small number of products contribute significant revenue, creating opportunities to focus on high-performing products.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Reduce UK Market Dependence

Expand targeted marketing campaigns into other European markets such as Germany, France, and EIRE.

**Expected impact:**  
More diversified revenue and reduced dependence on a single market.

### 2. Prepare for Seasonal Demand

Use historical sales patterns to plan inventory and marketing campaigns before peak-demand periods.

**Expected impact:**  
Better inventory planning and improved ability to capture seasonal demand.

### 3. Focus on High-Performing Products

Maintain sufficient inventory of high-performing products and analyze their characteristics to identify similar products with growth potential.

**Expected impact:**  
Improved product availability and potential revenue growth.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis shows that the e-commerce business generated strong overall revenue but is highly concentrated in the UK market.

The findings suggest three major opportunities:

**Revenue diversification + Seasonal planning + High-performing product optimization**

These recommendations can help the business reduce market risk, improve inventory planning, and identify opportunities for revenue growth.
